# Privacy Policy
*Effective Date: 6th June, 2025*

**hype.pass**, developed by **hype.**, is a secure offline password and card manager application. We take the privacy and protection of user data very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines the nature of data handled by the application and the measures taken to ensure its security. By using hype.pass, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. No Internet Access

hype.pass is designed to function entirely offline. The application does **not** request or use any internet access permissions. As a result:

- No user data is transmitted over the internet.
- The app does not connect to any servers.
- There is no collection, sharing, or syncing of data to any external entity or cloud service.

All user data remains strictly local to the user’s device.

---

## 2. No Access to External Storage

hype.pass does **not** request access to the device’s external storage (such as files, media, or shared storage). All data is securely stored in the app’s **internal storage**, which is sandboxed and inaccessible to other apps.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Encryption

All password and card data is stored using **Room Database**, a secure local database architecture within Android. Data stored in the app is:

- **Encrypted** before being saved.
- Only accessible through the app interface after proper authentication.
- Isolated from other apps and systems.

This ensures that even if a device is compromised, the stored data cannot be interpreted without the proper decryption credentials.

---

## 4. Authentication and App Security

To enhance user data protection, hype.pass uses:

- An **app-specific PIN** set by the user.
- The device’s **biometric authentication** methods (such as fingerprint or facial recognition) to unlock the app.

These layers of security ensure that only authorized users can access the application and its stored data.

---

## 5. Backup and Restore

hype.pass provides a local backup and restore feature. The following applies:

- Backups are stored in the device’s `Documents/hype.pass` directory.
- Backup files are **encrypted using the user’s app PIN**.
- To restore a backup, the user must provide the correct PIN used during the encryption process.

This ensures that even if someone gains access to the backup file, it cannot be used without the original credentials.

---

## 6. No Third-Party Dependencies or Tracking

hype.pass does **not** include any third-party libraries, SDKs, or analytics tools. There are:

- No advertisements.
- No behavioral tracking.
- No data sharing with third parties of any kind.

The app is entirely self-contained and built solely with native Android components.

---

## 7. Screenshot Blocking

To prevent unauthorized visual capture of sensitive information, hype.pass disables screenshots within the app. This is an additional layer of protection to safeguard your data from screen-based attacks or misuse.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

hype.pass is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Users are encouraged to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how their data is protected.
